**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer: maps

- **Sanctuary statue:** no more false waterfall ribbon over the Garuda statue, and the statue, rock walls, stone floor
  and wood show their own textures again.
- **Museum and Shambhala:** waterfall ribbons that PS3 hides are no longer drawn.
- **Crisp blends:** hard-edged texture blends on rubble and wood are sharp again on about 28 maps.
- **Yemen and London:** the wall blends in Yemen and the planter blends in London look as on PS3.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer: fixes

- **Co-op Arena on Village** no longer crashes, a crash in the middle of a match is fixed, and the cash popup for a
  co-op kill shows again. Thanks to 33martyr (PR 20)!

## Uncharted 2 multiplayer

- **Scoreboards** work better (work in progress), plus new multiplayer debugging. Thanks to Tha14 (PR 21)!

After the update the launcher converts the changed Uncharted 3 multiplayer maps again once; your progress is kept.
