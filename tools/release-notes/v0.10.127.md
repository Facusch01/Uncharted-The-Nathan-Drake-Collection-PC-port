**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer

- **Joining a match in progress spawns your player** (issue 90). The late joiner now goes the PS3 way: straight into
  the match, then the host lets it spawn.
- **Party members load the host's map** (issue 92). Before, a member could load another map than the host (for
  example Trainwreck while the host played Village). Everyone in a party needs this version.
- **Received invites show in the Invites tab** (issue 91), where you can join the party or decline. The "you were
  invited" notice now comes once the invite is stored, so the list has it.
- **Speaker icon while someone talks** (issue 81): in the party list (your own row while you talk, a muted icon for
  players you muted) and in the match's scoreboard and player markers.

Each of these needs at least two players to try. Please report how they work in their issues, with both players'
`boot.log`.

Still being worked on: freezes in custom games, matchmaking and Fort co-op (issue 55), lag and teleporting players
(issue 56), the broken enemy poses on guests (issue 58) and guests not throwing grenades (issue 59). Please attach your
`boot.log` and any `hang-*.txt` from the `analysis\big3-ps4_Shipping` folder to the issue.
