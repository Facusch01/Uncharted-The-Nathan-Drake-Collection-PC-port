**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 co-op with more than one guest (helper v7.241)

- **When one player's game freezes, the others no longer crash with it.** The other games kept queueing updates for
  the frozen player until they ran out of network memory.
- From 0.10.139: network events no longer go missing (guests missed enemies, box progress, checkpoints and
  cutscenes, and players could end up at different stages). All players should update.
- From 0.10.137 / 0.10.138: a single missing network event no longer stalls the game.

Still being worked on: enemies on guests that sometimes fly around or T-pose, a rare freeze in co-op, map lighting on
Facility and a few other maps, freezes in custom games, matchmaking and Fort co-op (issue 55), lag and teleporting
players (issue 56). Please attach your `boot.log` and any `hang-*.txt` / `u3mp-freeze.txt` from the
`analysis\big3-ps4_Shipping` folder to the issue.
