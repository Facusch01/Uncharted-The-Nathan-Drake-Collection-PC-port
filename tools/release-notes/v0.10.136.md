**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer import (recipe 1.30.0)

- **Maps are converted right the first time.** The fixes that used to run as separate steps after the conversion
  (shadow-only blockers, dark lightmap triangles, the Facility far shell, Fort stone steps, London sun, Shade Arena
  spawners, HUD icon mip levels, PS3 blend maps from normal maps) are now rules inside the map and material
  converter. The import goes from 50 to 37 steps and makes the same files.
- **Closer to PS3 on many maps:** soft layer blends (for example the Facility ship), water that reflects its
  surroundings again (Museum fountain and other pools), see-through cut-outs on gates, grilles and lattices, and
  shadow blockers that no longer stop rain/snow cover or reflections where retail PS4 lets them through (29 maps).
- The update reruns only the steps whose output changed.
- Keep the game closed while the import runs: some steps refuse to change files while it is open.

## Uncharted 2 multiplayer

- Online play follows the PS3 protocol more closely, with several small fixes (thanks Tha14).

Still being worked on: map lighting on Facility and a few other maps, freezes in custom games, matchmaking and Fort
co-op (issue 55), lag and teleporting players (issue 56). Please attach your `boot.log` and any `hang-*.txt` /
`u3mp-freeze.txt` from the `analysis\big3-ps4_Shipping` folder to the issue.
