**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Online, Uncharted 3 and Uncharted 2

- **The friend list in game stays current.** A game started from the launcher now always plays through the launcher's
  RPCN account, signed in or not yet. Friends you add, accept or remove in the launcher, and friends going online or
  offline, show up in game right away, without restarting the game or the launcher. The launcher no longer pauses
  while a game runs, so Add friend, Connect and Disconnect keep working during play.
- **Disconnect in the launcher during multiplayer:** the launcher now tells the game it signed out. Uncharted 2 shows
  its network-error message and leaves multiplayer. Uncharted 3 follows with its next multiplayer update.

## Uncharted 3 splitscreen (issue 31, work in progress)

- With keyboard + mouse as player 2, a key held while they change hands no longer joins and leaves player 2 again and
  again, and player 2's input no longer reaches player 1.
- Matches still start with one player and one view; this is being worked on.

Still being worked on: freezes in custom games, matchmaking and Fort co-op (issue 55), lag and teleporting players
(issue 56) and splitscreen (issue 31). Please attach your `boot.log` and any `hang-*.txt` / `u3mp-freeze.txt` from the
`analysis\big3-ps4_Shipping` folder to the issue.
