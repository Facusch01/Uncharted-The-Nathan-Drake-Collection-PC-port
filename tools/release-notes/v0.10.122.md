**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer and co-op

- **Co-op no longer freezes when a job waits for ever** (Syria and Yemen co-op froze now and then). When a job has
  waited 4 s for work that nothing is left to do, with every queue empty and no new frame for 3 s, the wait is
  ended so the game goes on. Thanks to Facusch01 (PR 63)!
- **Steadier player movement:** each player's network updates now go out every 50 ms. Before, they alternated between
  50 and 67 ms at 60 fps (issue 56).
- **For the co-op enemy fix (issue 58):** enemies on a guest's screen still get broken poses. This version writes
  `U3MP npc probe` lines into the guest's `boot.log`; please attach a guest's log from a co-op game to issue 58.

## From 0.10.119 to 0.10.121

- Guests see the enemies in co-op (issue 58), every player's character look reaches everyone (issue 57), no crash in
  matches with three or four players and no crash at start (issue 55).

Still being worked on: lag and teleporting players (issue 56), the broken enemy poses on guests (issue 58) and guests
not throwing grenades (issue 59). Please attach your `boot.log` and any `hang-*.txt` from the
`analysis\big3-ps4_Shipping` folder to the issue.
